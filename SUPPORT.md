# Support — Organize Files

Need help with **Organize Files**? We are happy to assist.

## Contact

- **Email:** razvan.gutulov@outlook.com
- **Publisher:** Razvan Constantin Gutulov

When you write, please include:

- Your operating system (Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, or iOS) and its version
- The app version (shown in the app's About / License screen)
- The store you installed from (Microsoft Store, Google Play, Apple App Store, or a side-loaded build)
- A clear description of the problem and the steps to reproduce it

We aim to reply within **2–3 business days**.

## Before contacting support

- Most actions run in **dry-run** (preview) mode first — no files are moved or deleted until you turn dry-run off and confirm.
- Always keep a **verified backup** before any execute run that moves or deletes files.
- The in-app documentation and assistant answer many common questions offline.
